# Brief: a centre-screen app that records the trip — speed (and more) — in the style of the car's own trip page

Audience: one agent, package **TG**. It designs the app end to end — specification, data study, mockups, a feasibility prototype on the bench — and does **not** ship it. The lead reads the result with the owner, then a build round follows.

## 0. What the owner wants (2026-11-18)

The car's own centre-screen page **"Дані про поїздку"** (trip data) shows, since the trip started: current consumption, average consumption, average speed, Auto Start/Stop time, electric distance, and a **graph of consumption over distance** (two windows: 0–10 km and 10–100 km, coloured by drive mode SPORT / ECO PRO / COMFORT, the average as a horizontal line with its value at the right), with the trip's time and distance under it. A screenshot of the owner's car is at `reference/hu/01_trip_data_page.png` (git-ignored; 1920 × 720; the profile picture in the status bar is blacked out; never copy its pixels into a tracked file).

The owner: *"I miss speed here. I want my own app in the same style — not only for fuel but recording speed too. There must be a switch for what we read."*

So: **a centre-screen app** (not the cluster), the same page grammar as the car's trip page, that **records** values over the trip itself and plots the one the driver chooses with a switch: **speed** first, fuel consumption as the car shows it, and whatever else the data allows (see §3).

## 1. Rules

- **Branch and worktree:** `git worktree add <main checkout>/.claude/worktrees/tg -b trip-graph/tg main`; symlink `reference` and `bake/work` (`git status --porcelain` must not list them). Commit on your branch. **No push, no version bump, no change to `apps/f30_cluster`, `apps/twin_dial`, `apps/driver_profiles`, `apps/signal_probe`.** A new app goes to `apps/trip_graph/` (working id `trip_graph`, working title "Поїздка+"; propose a better one).
- **No car** for you (no ssh). The lead holds the car's logs; the material you may read is `reference/car/2026-11-18-collect1/` (git-ignored, read-only; numbers only may leave it).
- **Not ours to ship:** no maker's name, logo, font or bitmap; the car's page is a reference for layout and behaviour, **our own drawing** replaces every element (fonts: the OFL ones in `tools/fonts-src/` — Noto Sans, Saira — or the kit's; pictograms our own, `shared/icons.js` / `shared/twin_icons.js` style). No pixel of the screenshot in a tracked file.
- **Read-only signals only:** `r` with a non-zero getter id, `s` for notifier-only; `tools/signals/check_signals.py <catalogue> <app.json>` must pass (catalogue `reference/car/2026-11-15/someip/someip-interfaces.json`). Never a setter, never a method with side effects, never a write to the car.
- **Privacy:** the app records speed and similar values over time and distance. **Never** a coordinate, a road name, a heading or anything that locates the car; no VIN or personal data. Records stay on the car in the app's own data folder; the log carries numbers only. State the retention (how many trips, how long) and give the driver a "clear history" action.
- **The car runs Node 8.16** (no `?.`, `??`, object spread, optional catch binding); an OAP app that exits is counted as a crash (never an uncaught exception); an app keeps running in the background after its screen is closed (it can record while the driver uses other screens) — confirm this from the kit and the platform notes (`.claude/skills/mgu-app-dev/`, `kit/`, `docs/`).
- **No browser automation**; bench ports 8080 and 8150 are the owner's, use 8800+; no process you cannot stop; Python via `UV_CACHE_DIR=<main checkout>/bake/work/.uvcache uv run -q --no-project --with … python -I <script>`; no home-directory path in a tracked file.
- **Evidence labels:** verified (measured, or seen in the car's logs), hypothesis, proposal.
- **Outputs:** `docs/trip-graph/SPEC.md`, `docs/trip-graph/TG-REPORT.md`, data in `design/trip-graph/*.json`, mockups under `bake/work/trip-graph/mockups/` (git-ignored) with a contact sheet, tools in `tools/trip-graph/`, the prototype in `apps/trip_graph/` and its tests in `bench/test-trip-graph*.js`. Finish with a short final message: branch, last commit, the choices for the owner.

## 2. What is known already (use it)

- **The centre screen:** the MGU App Kit builds RHMI screens from `app.json` ("screens"), and an app may draw a **GPU canvas** on the centre screen (see `.claude/skills/mgu-app-dev/references/app-structure.md`, `kit/`, the bench `node kit/tools/emu.js <app>` and `bench/`). f30_cluster's centre screen uses BMW's native menu plus a drawn gallery (`shared/gallery.js`, `docs/UI-NATIVE-REPORT.md`): read how a drawn screen is made, its size, its frame rate, how touch / controller input reaches it, and its limits.
- **Signals seen on this car on 2026-11-18** (`reference/car/2026-11-18-collect1/`, the probe app's log `apps/signal_probe/signal_probe.log`, Twin Dial's `apps/twin_dial/twin_dial.log`):
  - speed (as f30_cluster and Twin Dial read it) — arrives;
  - the trip computer: `KombiIF.statusBordcomputerAverage` (`speed1`, `cons1`: **factor 0.1**, verified against the car's page: raw 137 = 13.7 km/h, raw 155 = 15.5 l/100 km), `statusBordcomputerTravelData` (hours, minutes, **way in km, factor 1**: raw 19 = 19.2 km), `statusBordcomputerTravelStart`;
  - **the car's own segment history** — the probe's `P4 bc-segment` lines: `segment`, `trigger` (e.g. `INTERVALFINALIZED`), `durDrive`, `durMoving`, `distance`, `speedDrive`, `speedMoving`, `fuel` — and `P4 bc-all2` (`ResetLogic3`-style trips: `option` INDIVIDUAL / REFUEL / …, `speedAvg`, `consAvgScale/Digit`, trip day / hour / minute, reset time). **This is very likely what feeds the car's consumption graph.** Find its interface and field in `apps/signal_probe/fields.js` and the catalogue, its scaling (compare with the page: 19.2 km, 1:28:19, 15.5 l/100 km, 13.7 km/h), the segment length, how many segments are kept, and whether it can give speed per segment for free.
  - drive mode (COMFORT / SPORT / ECO PRO) — arrives (f30_cluster reads it) — for colouring the graph by mode as the car does;
  - odometer, fuel level and range — arrive;
  - Auto Start/Stop — f30_cluster reads MSA.
- **Twin Dial's research** on the trip computer: `docs/research3/MK-REPORT.md`, `shared/twin_tokens.js` (`tripValues`; its ResetLogic3 path reads an array as a struct — a known bug), CP's `docs/car/CP-REPORT.md`.

## 3. Work

1. **The reference page, measured** (`reference/hu/01_trip_data_page.png`): the header, the left column (label, values with pictograms: current and average consumption, average speed, Auto Start/Stop time, electric distance), the graph (axes, gridlines, the two windows 0–10 km and 10–100 km, the fill by drive mode and its colours, the average line and its label, the cursor dot), the footer (stopwatch + time, distance), the left "back" arrow, the selected item's orange frame. Positions, sizes, colours, fonts' sizes — in `design/trip-graph/reference_page.json`. This is what "the same style" means; reproduce the grammar with our own drawing.
2. **What can be recorded** — a table of candidate series, each with its source field, rate, scaling, status on this car (arrives / unseen), and whether the car keeps history of it or we must record it ourselves:
   - speed (our own sampling, e.g. 1 s; per distance step and per time step; average, maximum),
   - consumption (the car's segment history if usable; else our own from the fuel level or the instantaneous value if one exists),
   - drive mode (for colouring), engine rpm, coolant / oil temperature, outside temperature, boost (car_vitals saw it), altitude if a field exists (unseen), Auto Start/Stop time.
   Pick a first set for the switch (proposal: **Швидкість / Витрата / Оберти / Температура**) and say why.
3. **The specification** (`docs/trip-graph/SPEC.md`): the screens (the main trip page with the switch; a history of past trips; settings), the switch's form (an RHMI selector, a toggle row, or tabs on the drawn page — whichever the platform supports well on the centre screen and with the controller), the graph's axes per series (distance windows like the car's, or time), the colour by drive mode, the summary values per series (speed: average, maximum, time above a threshold; consumption: current, average), what "trip" means (since the car's own trip start, since ignition, since a manual reset in our app — propose and justify), recording while the screen is closed, storage format, size and retention, the "clear history" action, behaviour with no data, units (km/h, l/100 km; mph if the car says so), day / night colours, the log lines (numbers only).
4. **Mockups** (1920 × 720, the centre screen's size — confirm it): the main page for each series of the switch (speed first: a plausible city trip with stops; motorway), the switch itself in each state, an empty trip, a long trip (the 10–100 km window), the history list, settings, night. Draw them with Python / Pillow in our own grammar; put the reference screenshot beside one of them **only** in a git-ignored contact sheet for comparison.
5. **Feasibility prototype** on the bench: `apps/trip_graph` that records speed (and the car's segment history if it is readable) into its data folder and draws the main page with the switch, driven by injected signals; a test that records a synthetic trip and checks the summary values and the stored file; `check_signals.py` passes; Node 8 syntax.
6. **Report** (`TG-REPORT.md`): what the platform allows on the centre screen (drawn canvas or RHMI only, its frame rate, input), the data findings (the segment history's meaning and scale), what is verified and what needs the car (which probe set / which log line would confirm each), the choices for the owner, and the work estimate for a real build.
